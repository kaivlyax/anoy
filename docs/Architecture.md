# ANOY Architecture

## Vision
ANOY is a modern community and collaboration platform designed to bring students, developers, creators, and communities together in a single ecosystem.
The platform combines secure communication, community management, project collaboration, AI-powered assistance, and real-time interactions into one application.
Unlike traditional messaging applications, ANOY focuses on building communities where users can communicate, collaborate on projects, share knowledge, and showcase their work.
## Objectives
-Build a secure communicatio platform
-Enable real-time messaging and collaboration.
-Allow communities to customize their own spaces.
-Provide AI-powered productivity tools.
-Support voice and video communication.
-Create an extensible modular architecture.
-Deploy the application across Web, Android, and iOS.
